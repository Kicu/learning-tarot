A Map of the 78 Cards

The full inventory of a Rider–Waite–Smith deck, arranged for sorting, counting and finding strays. Print it and keep it under the deck.

The grid

BlockCountContents
Trumps (Major Arcana) 22 0 The Fool, then I–XXI, each carrying a numeral and a title. In the game, the fifth suit that beats the other four.
Suit cards (Minor Arcana) 56 Four suits × fourteen: Ace–10, then Page, Knight, Queen, King. Remove the four Knights and the structure of the modern 52-card pack remains.

The element column is doctrine, not history: it comes from the Golden Dawn's Book T (1888), following Lévi's 1855 scheme. Everything else in the table is documented card-making lineage running back through the Latin suits to the Mamluk pack in the Topkapı Palace Museum.

Authored · 1909

Strength at VIII and Justice at XI is Waite's swap — decks before 1909, and some after, number them the other way around. The Fool's 0 is a rules artifact rather than a mystery: in the game he was the "excuse," playable in place of following suit. Both stories are in lesson 1.
