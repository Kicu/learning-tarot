Glosariusz — the Polish Vocabulary of Tarot

How Polish tarot literature names the deck, its parts, and the act of reading. Polish terms first, common variants in brackets, English alongside — so you can move between a Polish conversation and an English source without stumbling.

Talia i jej części · The deck and its parts

Wielkie Arkana (Arkana Wielkie) Major Arcana, trumps The 22 trumps. Grammatically plural — see the language note below.
Małe Arkana (Arkana Małe) Minor Arcana, suit cards The 56 suit cards.

Language note · PWN

In standard Polish, arkana is a plurale tantum meaning "the secrets of a craft, known only to experts" (arkana sztuki), from the Latin arcanum. Tarot writing back-forms a singular from it, so you will meet a single card called arkan — say trzynasty arkan for trump XIII — even though general dictionaries list only the plural. One more distinction worth keeping: tarot names the divinatory deck, while the Central-European trick-taking game is known in Polish as tarok.

Polish publishing has never settled on one set. Buławy is the most common headword (Polish Wikipedia leads with it), but decks translated from English often say Różdżki, a direct rendering of Wands. Likewise Denary keeps the old coin name — the same denari lineage as the Italian game suits — while Pentakle follows Waite's renaming. Expect any of the variants in any Polish book.

Label the elements

As on the deck map: the suit–element pairing is Golden Dawn doctrine (Book T, 1888), not card-making history. Polish sources print it as standard because they inherit it from the same occult lineage.

Numbering follows the deck

Marseille-derived Polish lists put Sprawiedliwość at VIII and Moc at XI; your RWS deck carries Waite's 1909 swap, so Siła is VIII and Sprawiedliwość XI. The story is in lesson 1.

Praktyka czytania · The practice of reading

Polish (variants)EnglishNotes
rozkład (układ) spread Both the layout pattern and the dealt reading; rozkład z trzech kart is the three-card spread.
Krzyż Celtycki Celtic Cross The ten-position spread from Waite's Pictorial Key, as popular in Polish practice as everywhere else.
pozycja position A slot in the spread; its assigned meaning constrains the card that lands there.
tasowanie shuffling Verb tasować.
stawiać karty (stawianie tarota) to read the cards The everyday idiom — literally "to lay the cards." A reader may be called tarocista / tarocistka.
kwerent (pytający) querent The person asking; borrowed into Polish exactly as English borrowed it from Latin.
sygnifikator significator The card chosen to stand for the querent, e.g. in the Celtic Cross.
karta odwrócona reversed card Upside-down in the deal; upright is w pozycji prostej. Whether reversals are worth using at all is a live question — lesson 7 will take it up.
znaczenie proste / odwrócone upright / reversed meaning The two meaning entries Polish guidebooks list per card, mirroring Waite's format.
