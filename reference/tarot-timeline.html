The Three Layers of a Tarot Card

Every card in a Rider–Waite–Smith deck is a stack of three eras. Naming the layer tells you which claim you are making — and how much weight it can carry.

The layer decoder

LayerDatesWhat it put into the cardStatus
1 · The game 1440s– The 78-card structure. Four suits of 14, plus a fifth run of 22 trionfi (trumps) that beat the suits. Trump numbers exist to settle who wins a trick. Subjects drawn from ordinary late-medieval imagery: virtues, estates of society, Triumphs, the Dance of Death. Documented
2 · Occult overlay 1781–1888 Divinatory meanings. Egyptian origin story. Hebrew letters, Kabbalah's Tree of Life, the four classical elements, astrological rulers. Reversals. Spreads. Asserted
3 · Waite & Smith 1909 A narrative scene on every one of the 56 suit cards — the single change that makes the deck readable by picture. Deliberate esoteric props. Justice and Strength swapped to VIII/XI. The specific faces, colours and gestures you actually interpret. Authored
Use this sheet like this

When you justify a reading, say which layer you are standing on. "Focused will" is layer 2–3. "A fairground conjuror, maybe a con man" is layer 1. Both are legitimately in the card. Only one of them is 15th-century.

Timeline

DateEventLayer
1440Earliest known written record of trump cards: the notary Giusto Giusti records commissioning a pack of trionfi in Florence, painted with the crest of Sigismondo Malatesta. Found by Franco Pratesi and Thierry Depaulis; pushed the earliest date back from Ferrara 1442.1
1442Ferrara court account books record payments for carte da trionfi — the long-standing earliest reference before the Florence find.1
c. 1450The Visconti-Sforza deck is hand-painted in Milan, attributed to Bonifacio Bembo. Roughly 74 cards survive, mostly at the Morgan Library and in Bergamo.1
c. 1500–1700The game spreads to France, Switzerland, Germany, Austria. Woodblock printing standardises the imagery into the pattern later called the Tarot de Marseille. No divinatory use is recorded anywhere in this period.1
1781Antoine Court de Gébelin, in Le Monde primitif vol. VIII, asserts the cards are surviving pages of an Egyptian Book of Thoth, smuggled past the burning of Alexandria. He offers no evidence. Egyptian hieroglyphs would not be deciphered for another forty years.2
1783–85Jean-Baptiste Alliette, writing as Etteilla, publishes the first method for reading tarot and later the first deck built for divination rather than play. Professional tarot reading begins here.2
1855Éliphas Lévi, in Dogme et Rituel de la Haute Magie, welds the 22 trumps to the 22 letters of the Hebrew alphabet and the Kabbalistic Tree of Life — assigning Aleph to the Magician. The argument is essentially that both sets number 22.2
1888The Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn is founded in London. It systematises tarot correspondences: element and astrological ruler per card, trumps mapped to paths on the Tree of Life. Almost every "traditional" meaning in print today descends from this syllabus.2
Dec 1909William Rider & Son, London, publishes the deck designed by A. E. Waite and drawn by Pamela Colman Smith — both Golden Dawn members. Drawn April–October 1909. Smith is paid a flat fee of about £50, no royalties.3
1910/11Waite's companion book The Pictorial Key to the Tarot appears, with Smith's plates. It remains the primary document for what the deck's imagery was meant to convey.3
1951Smith dies in poverty in Cornwall, her work uncredited on the deck's name. Her effects are sold to pay debts.3
1980Michael Dummett publishes The Game of Tarot, establishing the Italian game origin from archival evidence and demonstrating that no occult or divinatory use predates the 18th century.—

Four things people get wrong

The common claimWhat the evidence says
Tarot came from ancient Egypt.Invented by Court de Gébelin in 1781 with no evidence. The cards are northern Italian, 1440s.
Tarot came from the Roma.A 19th-century addition to the Egyptian story. No documentary support.
The trumps encode an ancient initiatory system.The trump subjects are stock late-medieval Christian and civic imagery. The esoteric system was mapped on after 1781.
Playing cards descend from tarot.Backwards. Four-suited packs reached Europe from the Islamic world in the 1370s; tarot is those packs plus a fifth suit of trumps, added ~70 years later.

Vocabulary

Trionfi"Triumphs." The 15th-century word for the trump cards, and the origin of English "trump."
Trumps / Major ArcanaThe 22 cards that beat the suits. "Major Arcana" is Etteilla-era occult vocabulary; "trumps" is what they were called for 340 years.
Suit cards / Minor ArcanaThe 56 cards in four suits of 14: ace–10 plus Page, Knight, Queen, King.
Pip cardA number card showing only repeated suit signs and no scene — how all 40 number cards looked before 1909.
RWSRider–Waite–Smith. Rider published, Waite directed, Smith drew all 78.
Layer 2 is not worthless

Labelling the occult layer as invented does not mean discarding it. It is a coherent, richly developed symbolic language, and it is the language your deck was drawn in — Waite and Smith were Golden Dawn initiates putting Golden Dawn ideas on the cards. Read it as authored symbolism, the way you would read the iconography of a Renaissance painting. Just do not call it history.
