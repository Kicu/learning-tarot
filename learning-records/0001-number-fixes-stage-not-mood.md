# The number fixes the stage, never the mood — surfaced via the Three of Swords

After lesson 4, Mateusz read the chapter table as if the number also carried the card's emotional valence ("3 = the pairing produces something new" felt incompatible with the Three of Swords being heartbreak). Corrected by walking row 3 across all four suits (Established Strength / Abundance / Sorrow / Material Works): the stage is constant, and the suit's material decides whether that stage is good news — the same rule the lesson stated for the tens, which he hadn't yet generalised to other rows.

## Evidence

He asked the question himself, with the right pieces already in hand (quoted the chapter gloss verbatim, knew the card meaning) — the gap was applying the tens-row principle everywhere, not missing knowledge.

## Implications

- When teaching individual cards later, lead with "what does this suit's material look like at this stage?" before giving the meaning — he responds to computed meanings, not asserted ones.
- The Two → Three of Swords transition (truce → the truth lands) worked as the explanatory device; within-suit N→N+1 walks are a good tool for him.
- Expect the same stumble at the courts (rank fixes posture, suit fixes register) — pre-empt it in lesson 5.
