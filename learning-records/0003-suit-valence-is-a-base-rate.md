# Suit valence is a base rate, not part of the message

Carrying [[0002-swords-mind-that-cuts]] into reading practice, Mateusz proposed that a drawn Swords card answers in the domain of mind and words *and* "will most likely be about hard truths and argument." The first half is the correct model (the suit re-asks the question in its register); the second half was corrected from rule to base rate — Swords does skew dark across its Book T titles, but a specific card is read from its number's stage, never from the suit's average, and the Six or Ace of Swords read as "probably conflict" would be a misread.

## Evidence

He generated the model himself and asked for confirmation, which is application-level engagement; the correction landed on top of the already-held "stage from number, verdict computed" rule from [[0001-number-fixes-stage-not-mood]].
