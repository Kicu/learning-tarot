## Teaching preferences (stated 2026-07-30)
- **Prose style (added 2026-08-01, v0.2 applied 2026-08-15):** `writing-style.md` at the repo root governs all lesson prose. Flowing sentences over staccato fragments; avoid "Here is X and it is not Y" and "This is Z: it does A, it does B" patterns. v0.2 additions: no suspense/reveal sentence structure (name the thing first, then describe it step by step — no "…and it does", "Now watch what…", "meet the limit that matters"), and keep parallels/analogies to a minimum ("less convoluted, more straightforward" — his words, 2026-08-15; lessons 4 and 5 were rephrased accordingly). When revising existing text, change no more than ~25–30% of it. **Word-choice test (2026-08-15, from his critique of one sentence):** every sentence should be one "a normal person would say" — never pick a word because it echoes a motif elsewhere in the course. Retired pet names: "the machine/machinery" (→ formula, system), "streams" in lesson prose (→ traditions, sources), "prices the plot / what the plot costs" (rule is now "the number sets the stakes" everywhere), "weather" outside lesson 3 (→ mood). One coherent, self-explanatory metaphor is fine; a codename needing cross-reference is not. Lesson 3 still contains "machinery/machine" in its source box — not yet swept. 2026-08-19: he requested a style sweep of lesson 7 right after delivery — the first draft had reveal-structure sentences ("What neither lesson said is…", "the stretch's quiet surprise"), a compressed parallel aphorism ("rule that grows things against rule that holds things"), staccato fragments ("Out loud, once."), and cross-lesson motif words (seam, costume change, redecoration); all rewritten. ## Callout convention (structural, do not drop)
Two visually distinct callouts carry the labelling requirement:
- `.callout.documented` — claims backed by dated documentary evidence. Cite the source.
- `.callout.invented` — claims that are tradition, invention, or assertion. Say who asserted it and when.

## Canonical naming decisions
Not yet promoted to `GLOSSARY.md` — glossary entries are only added once Mateusz can *use* a term correctly. These are my consistency rules in the meantime:

| Use | Avoid | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Rider–Waite–Smith (RWS) | Rider-Waite | Smith drew all 78 cards; standard modern practice credits her. |
| trumps / Major Arcana | Majors (informal only) | "Trumps" is the historically accurate term and reveals the game origin. |
| suit cards / Minor Arcana | Minors, pips | "Pip" is reserved for the *unillustrated* pre-1909 number cards — a real distinction. |
| trionfi | tarocchi | The 15th-c. word in the earliest documents. |
