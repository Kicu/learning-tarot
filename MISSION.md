# Mission: Tarot and Tarot Symbolism

## Why
Mateusz wants to sit down with a friend, deal a spread from his own Rider–Waite–Smith deck, and give a reading that is coherent, insightful, and *justified* — where he can point at the picture and explain which tradition put that symbol there and what it was meant to convey. The same literacy doubles as a decoder ring for tarot imagery in art, film and games, and as a structured prompt for thinking through his own situations.

## Success looks like
- Deal a three-card and a Celtic Cross spread from memory, and read it aloud without a guidebook.
- For any of the 78 cards, name its core meaning *and* cite the visual evidence in the image that supports it.
- Explain where tarot came from, and separate documented history from 18th-century occult invention.
- Spot and decode a tarot reference in a film, album cover, or novel.
- Use a spread on himself as a reflection tool without pretending it predicts anything.

## Constraints
- Owns a physical Rider–Waite–Smith deck; all practice should use the real cards in hand.
- Wants the esoteric system taught **properly, but always labelled** — traditional/occult claims presented as the tradition's internal logic, clearly flagged against documented history. Never blur the two.
- Complete beginner. No prior tarot, occult, or Kabbalah background assumed.
- Lessons must be short and finishable in one sitting.

## Out of scope
- Astrology, Kabbalah, or ceremonial magic as standalone subjects — only touched where they explain a card.
- Non-RWS systems (Marseille pip reading, Thoth) beyond the historical contrast needed to see what RWS added.
- Professional/paid reading, ethics of client work, business of tarot.
